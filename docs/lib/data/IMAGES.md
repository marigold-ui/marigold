# Demo venue images

How the venue photos in `public/venues/` were made, so the set can be extended in the same look. They are AI-generated with Gemini, one fresh chat per image and no reference image, and every venue in them is invented (see the comment at the top of `venues.ts` and /impressum).

## How to make a new one

1. Start a new Gemini chat. Do not upload a reference image: it made results flatter and pulled every image toward the reference.
2. Send one prompt: the venue line, then the base, the camera and light blocks for the shot, then the tail. Mix the light and camera so a new image does not repeat its neighbours in the list.
3. Reject it if it shows readable text or signs, smeared faces, missing furniture (tables without chairs), seats that do not face the stage, or HDR glow.
4. Export from the original download at 800 px wide, WebP: `cwebp -q 72 -m 6 -sharp_yuv -resize 800 0 -metadata none in.jpeg -o out.webp`. The largest demo shows a photo about 400 px wide, so 800 px covers a 2× screen. The files come out at 30 to 125 KB.
5. Name the file after the venue (`/venues/<slug>.webp`) and set it as `image` in `venues.ts`.

Outdoor venues and clubs are shown with an event under way, the guests seen from behind and the side; formal and rustic venues are shown set up just before the doors open. That is how venues present themselves in their own listings: in use, not empty.

## Blocks

**Base** (every image)

> A professional marketing photograph for the venue's website and brochure, taken by an experienced hospitality and event photographer and lightly edited, a real photo, not a render. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 24 to 35 mm lens at eye level, verticals straight, carefully composed so the space and its atmosphere read at a glance.

**Camera** (optional, after the base)

- `V1` Eye level, down the room: nothing added.
- `V2` Raised, from a balcony or gallery: Seen from a raised position, a balcony or gallery at the back, looking down over the whole space.
- `V3` Three-quarter side angle: Seen from the side at a three-quarter angle, not straight down the centre line.
- `V4` From the stage, looking out: Seen from the stage looking out over the room towards the back.
- `V5` Tight, foreground detail: A closer, tighter framing: a few tables, seats or guests in the foreground with the stage behind, more detail and texture.

**Light**

- `IE1` Indoor event, warm room light: The mood comes from the room's own warm light, the stage spots, table candles and lamps, kept as the photographer found it, with a subtle bounced fill so the room and the audience stay readable. The performer is crisp, a hint of movement in the crowd. Rich but natural colour, one clean white balance, deep shadows that still hold detail.
- `IE2` Indoor event, cool stage wash: Cool, modern stage light: a blue and violet wash on the back wall, one clean white spot on the performer, the rest of the room dim and lit by the spill from the stage, the audience in cool silhouette. Crisp performer, natural skin tones in the spot.
- `IE3` Indoor event, blue dusk and warm lamps: Early evening: the windows still show a deep blue dusk outside, mixed with warm pendant lights and lamps inside, so the picture holds both blue and amber. Crisp performer, natural colour.
- `IE4` Indoor event, daytime: A daytime event: bright, even daylight through the windows for an afternoon show or launch, fresh neutral colours, pale walls and wood reading clean and airy, no candles needed.
- `OE1` Outdoor event, late afternoon: Late-afternoon sun low and to one side, warm but not orange, light falling across the stage and the audience, soft shadows, a clear sky with detail, everything crisp. Natural, slightly warm colour.
- `OE2` Outdoor event, midday summer: Bright midday summer sun, a deep blue sky with a few white clouds, short crisp shadows, saturated but natural greens, people in summer clothes and sunhats.
- `OE3` Outdoor event, overcast spring: A soft overcast spring day, even light with no hard shadows, fresh pale greens, a light grey sky, people in light jackets.
- `OE4` Outdoor event, blue hour: Blue hour just after sunset: the sky a deep even blue, festoon lights and the stage lights glowing warm, the audience lit by the stage.
- `OE5` Outdoor event, autumn: A crisp, clear autumn afternoon, trees in orange, red and yellow, low clear light, people in sweaters and scarves.
- `IS1` Set-up, event-night lighting: The room lit the way it looks on an event night, its own chandeliers, wall lights, house lights or table lights on, and where the room has windows, balanced with soft daylight so the windows keep their detail; the room glows warmly without looking artificial. Shot on a tripod at f/8, everything sharp, the room immaculately set.
- `IS2` Set-up, late-morning daylight: Bright late-morning daylight flooding through the windows, the room's own lights off or barely noticeable, airy and clean, neutral whites and natural materials, set for a daytime event. Shot on a tripod at f/8, everything sharp, the room immaculately set.
- `IS3` Set-up, coloured uplighting: Evening, with architectural uplighting washing the walls in one soft colour (deep teal, plum or amber) as set for a reception, cooler and more modern than candlelight, the tables lit by small pin spots. Shot on a tripod, everything sharp, the room immaculately set.
- `IS4` Set-up, stage light only: Moody and dark: only the stage lit and a few pin spots on the tables or seats, the rest of the room falling into deep shadow, dramatic contrast that still shows the architecture. Shot on a tripod, the room immaculately set.
- `OS1` Outdoor set-up, early evening: Soft early-evening light with string lights or lanterns just switched on, the set-up immaculate, a sky with gentle colour, natural tones.

**Tail, event**

> Polished like a professional's delivered edit but believable as a real photograph: natural materials and skin, no HDR halos, no oversaturation, no glow, no lens flare, no heavy vignette, no artificial bokeh. The guests are a mixed crowd of different ages and genders in casual and smart-casual clothes, engaged and enjoying the event, seen mostly from behind and in profile, candid, nobody posing for the camera. 4:3 landscape, main subject in the centre. No readable text, no logos, no signs.

**Tail, set-up**

> Polished like a professional's delivered edit but believable as a real photograph: natural materials and skin, no HDR halos, no oversaturation, no glow, no lens flare, no heavy vignette, no artificial bokeh. No guests yet; at most one or two staff, small and busy, making final touches. 4:3 landscape, main subject in the centre. No readable text, no logos, no signs.
