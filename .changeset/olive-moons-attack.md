docs(DST-1719): document the single-thumb form of the Slider `thumbLabels` prop

The prop was only described under "Ranges", so the single-thumb string form was undocumented even though a thumb label is the only way to say what a single-thumb slider's value refers to. Adds a "Labelling the thumbs" section covering both forms.
